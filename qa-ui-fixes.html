Audit fixes 1 to 4

Before is the live app (main). After is the fresh branch from today's main, running locally on localhost:8084. Nothing is committed or pushed yet.

1 · One header on every screen (F01, high)

Settings, Profile, Chat, What's new, Privacy and Your answers now use the same cream bar and Fredoka title as the tabs. One change in app/_layout.tsx, reusing the existing headerOptions(theme).

2 · Keyboard focus ring (F51, high)

Press Tab: the browser's thin blue ring becomes a 2 px ring in our primary green with a 2 px gap. Mouse and touch never show it. One CSS rule in public/index.html; the colour follows the theme.

3 · Back button name (F54, high)

No visual change. Screen readers said "index, back" or "(tabs), back"; they now say "Go back" on every stacked screen (checked on Settings, Profile, Chat, Add a pet, pet page).

4 · Placeholder text: your call (F52, high)

Placeholders were 50% opacity, 2.2:1 on white (WCAG asks 4.5:1). Going full textMuted passes easily but, as the right screen shows, the hints start to look like filled-in answers.
