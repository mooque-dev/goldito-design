A

Coach marks inside the app

A five-step tour that starts after “Try demo”. Each step dims the screen, spotlights one control and names the model behind it. Five steps: search, AI reply, request booking, treat check, daily note.

Why it could work

  • Teaches by doing, in the real app; the judge feels the product.
  • Model names sit right on the moment they act (routing, made visible).
  • Asse got the feedback “needs onboarding tooltips”; this answers it.

Why it might not

  • Every step needs a reliable state: the chips need the AI awake, the daily note needs a stay that demo accounts don't have.
  • Tours are skipped fast; judges hate being herded.
  • Touches many screens 8 days before the freeze.
Effort: 2 to 3 days (Minsik + us)Risk: highNeeds: a seeded stay

B

A judge panel beside the phone

Picked

Play the judge panel ▸

On desktop, the empty space next to the phone frame becomes a short guide: one line about Goldito, four “Show me” steps that jump the phone straight to each moment, the model behind each one, and a QR code for phones.

Why it works

  • It's ticket #111 (the side panel judges see), already P0 and assigned to us: no new scope.
  • The app's screens stay untouched; the panel only links into states that already exist.
  • Model chips on each step are the routing picture the judges asked for, with no separate diagram.
  • Works the same in split view, and steps tick off so the judge sees progress.

Watch out for

  • “Show me: the daily note” needs a stay in progress; without one it opens the Draft instead.
  • Shared demo accounts: two judges at once see each other's messages.
Effort: 1 day (panel) + small deep linksRisk: lowTicket: #111

C

The “aha” before sign-in

The welcome screen plays a 20-second, three-card story: Chloe asks, the AI drafts the reply, the treat check catches chicken. Then two big buttons drop the judge into the demo as either side.

Why it could work

  • The wow lands in the first 20 seconds, even if the judge never signs in.
  • Pure frontend, no data or AI calls, so it never fails or waits.
  • The same three cards can open the video and the Devpost gallery.

Why it might not

  • It tells instead of shows; judges might read it as a marketing page.
  • Changes the first screen everyone sees, including real owners after launch.
  • Doesn't help once the judge is inside the app.
Effort: 1 to 1.5 daysRisk: mediumReuse: video + Devpost images
