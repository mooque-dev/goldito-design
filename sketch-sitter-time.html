B · Mook's split screen · my pick

Four jobs at once, each with its minutes

After the owner's texts, the screen splits into four tiles, each a tiny loop of one job: texts going back and forth, photos picked, a report being typed, an alarm ringing. Each tile shows its minutes, then all four become the bar.

  1. 0:05 to 0:10.5 the cut as it is: the calm pet, the owner's texts, Chloe still typing
  2. 0:10.5 on "Send a photo!", the screen splits into four, all moving at once
  3. 0:10.5 to 0:13 texts, photos, the report and the alarms, each with its minutes
  4. 0:13 on "forty minutes", the four tiles become the 40 min bar
Narration stays as recordedThe tiles name the four jobs on screen, so nothing new has to be said. They arrive on "Send a photo!" and turn into the bar on "forty minutes a day".

Why I'd pick it: all four jobs are on screen long enough to read, the minutes are tied to each one, and the same four tiles can come back later as "With Goldito" (5, 3, 3, 2).
