Rules

One canvas120 × 120 viewBox. Artwork stays inside 8 to 112, so every piece reads the same size.
One outline3 px ink (#1B4332), round caps and joins, on every shape that has an edge.
Flat fills, nine coloursOnly the brand palette below. No gradients, no textures.
One ground shadowAn ink ellipse at 12% under each object (y 104). Nothing else casts shadow.
One small highlightA white sliver at 55 to 70% on round objects. Optional.
Front-facing, simpleOne object, centred, no perspective, no scene. A face only on pets.
  1. Safe area: the dashed box (8 to 112).
  2. Outline: the same 3 px ink line on the bowl, rim and kibble.
  3. Fills: terracotta, cream, bark.
  4. Ground shadow: the soft ellipse at the bottom.
  5. Detail: the paw on the bowl, in cream, no outline.

Where it comes from

SourceThe prototype's set (redesign/art.svg, Oct 6), drawn as placeholders for the Expressive look. The Figma Expressive screens use the same pieces (Snap, Diary timeline, Home stats, pet tiles).
Style familyOutlined flat stickers: thick even outline, flat brand fills, one contact shadow. The family friendly consumer apps use for spot art; it fits the gold coin logo and the pixel pets because all three are bold, flat and simple.
PaletteDESIGN.md brand colours (ink, mint, leaf, green) plus the warm accents from the Expressive colour blocks.
Honest statusPlaceholder quality, but consistent. Final art could replace any piece later if it keeps the id and the rules.
