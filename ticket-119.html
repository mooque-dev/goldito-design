Coat-color skins and pixel pets

Two P1 features that share one idea: the app looks like your pet. A coat photo picks one of seven skins (11.10), and Owner Home gets a small 8-bit room where Max or Mochi shows how the day is going (11.12). The art comes from the brand's pixel pets, so it matches the logo. Both assets are drawn and exported; try them below, then pick answers at the bottom.

Today, in the app

Skins: the contract is ready, the skins aren't

theme/themes.ts defines SkinColors: the 10 colours a skin may change (primary, primaryText, background, accent, surface, border, borderStrong, track, text, textMuted). Error, warning and success are left out on purpose, so a DANGER warning never blends into a skin. Only default and balanced exist; the comment says “Coat-color skins (6–8, from the designer) are added here in 11.10.”

Room: specced, not built

pet-status-room.ko.md asks for a room card on Owner Home: an 8-bit pet in five moods (happy, calm, tired, hungry, sleepy), a 2–4 frame idle, a HUD (fed, potty, mood, next task), tap → Activity. Sprites go in frontend/assets/pixel-pets/, which doesn't exist yet. Mochi has no breed in the demo data, so she uses the generic cat.

Which skin does a coat get?

The vision model only names the coat ({coat_colors[], pattern, confidence}); a fixed server rule picks the skin, so a photo's lighting can never produce an unreadable colour. Tap a coat.

The seven skins

Each coat skin is the coat's hue, darkened until text and buttons pass 4.5 : 1 on its own background. The 10 swatches are the SkinColors keys in order; the numbers are measured, not targets.

Sprites: five moods, two frames each

24 × 24 cells, the brand's faces with the gold bell tag. Each mood is a slow two-step idle: a blink, a breath, the Z of a sleeping pet coming and going. Reduce Motion shows the first frame only.

Exported for frontend/assets/pixel-pets/
  • <pet>-<mood>.png × 20: two frames side by side, drawn at 8× (384 × 192), transparent
  • The same at 1× in src/ (48 × 24) for the web with image-rendering: pixelated
  • pixel-pets.json: pets, moods, frame size 24, scale 8, 600 ms per frame
  • Pets: max (Maltese), dog (any other dog), cat (any cat; tabby), cat-tuxedo (if you pick it)
  • skins.ts: the six coat skins as SkinColors, to paste into themes.ts
  • sheet.png: the contact sheet
