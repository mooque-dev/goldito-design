Sitter calendar: next openings and a day page

When an owner looks at a sitter's profile, the month calendar fills the screen and a tapped day's hours land below the fold as one long sentence. Minsik's #150 already removed the M · A · N letters. Decided (Oct 18): B + D. The profile lists Chloe's next openings in plain words; the month opens as its own page; tapping a day opens a day page with big slot rows and a clear Back. Nothing slides or folds under the finger, and there are no hidden gestures, so it works for older owners and with a mouse. Options compared: ticket-120-options.html.

Drawn from owner/sitters/[sitterId].tsx and SlotCalendar.tsx on main (Oct 18), drawn full length. The red line is the bottom of a 402 × 874 phone, above the Ask / Book bar.

What each one costs
  • A · Roll up: the week keeps the hatch in view next to the words, and the slots sit right under it with no scroll. One new state in SlotCalendar plus a handle.
  • B · Sheet: the month never moves, but the sheet covers the very days you're comparing, and it's a second layer to close.
  • C · Month stays: no new behaviour; only the rows change. The fold problem stays.
My pick: A, with rows and the handle

It answers IQ-13 as written (roll up, readable slots, pull back down) and fits the one-column phone. The handle carries a text button, so it works with a mouse in the desktop frame, and it isn't a hidden gesture. Only the owner's view of a sitter rolls up; the sitter's own Schedule keeps the full month, where range dragging (#150) needs it.
