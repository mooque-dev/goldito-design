Side by side

What each one costs
  • A: the calmest row, and one place for everything else. But alerts are the product (“learn without asking”), and they move one tap away behind ≡, where a number doesn't say what it counts.
  • B: the team's menu for everything else, with the bell kept out. A new alert stays one tap away and the badge sits where its meaning is obvious.
  • C: no menu at all; the avatar opens Profile and Log out lives at the bottom of Settings. Fewest new parts to build, but Schedule and What's new stay two taps deep.
My pick: B

It keeps the team's idea (a menu that can grow: Settings, What's new, Help, Log out) without hiding the one thing owners open the app for. Same header for both roles: the sitter's Schedule moves into the menu, so the rows finally match.

Where Log out goes

Settings already exists (Profile → “Settings · What's New”) with the account, notifications, What's new, the version and Privacy, but no Log out. Put it at the bottom there as an outlined button, behind a “Log out?” sheet (as built in PR #178). In A and B it's also the last row of the menu.
