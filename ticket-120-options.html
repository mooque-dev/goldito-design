#120 Calendar options

How an owner reads Chloe's calendar on her profile. Pick an option, then tap through the phone. Every option avoids moving layouts and hidden gestures.

  • Words, not letters: Morning · Afternoon · Night with their hours.
  • Every action is a big labelled button (48 px or more). No drag, no pull, no long press.
  • Nothing slides or collapses under the finger.
  • Busy days look different without relying on color: they say "Busy" and can't be tapped.
